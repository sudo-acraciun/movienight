Delete your account

I Like Movies · Last updated 23 June 2026

You can permanently delete your I Like Movies account and the data tied to it at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

From inside the app (fastest)

Open the app and go to Settings → Delete Account, then confirm. Your account and its data are erased right away.

If you can't sign in

Email alexcraciun10@gmail.com from the address tied to your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm your request and remove your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account: your profile, ratings, reviews, notes, lists, watchlist, friend connections, household membership, messages to the assistant, and any photos you uploaded. Residual copies in encrypted backups are removed on the normal backup rotation (within 30 days).

See our Privacy Policy for full details on the data we hold.
